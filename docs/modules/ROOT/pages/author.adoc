= About the Author

Gareth Price is CTO at CorralData, an AI-powered business intelligence platform serving the healthcare industry, and the author of https://www.startupctobook.com/[_Applied Alchemy: The Startup CTO's Field Guide to Building Technology Companies from Nothing_].
He runs a team in which a growing share of the work is done by agents — a project manager that reports to him, a promise-keeper that checks whether he did what he said he would, a QA reviewer that belongs to his QA lead, and a bot whose job is onboarding the other bots — and he has made most of the mistakes this book describes, usually before lunch.

He came to the United States in his twenties and ran a small IT company that grew faster than his ability to delegate.
A book about organising a business around roles rather than people got him through it, and the idea that stuck — draw the finished org chart first, then fill the boxes — is the idea this book rebuilds for a team where some of the boxes are filled by software.

Before CorralData, Gareth was a technologist at The New York Times, where he worked on the Storylines team, and at Codecademy, where he built tools for the people who teach other people to code.
He studied artificial intelligence at the University of Manchester, back when that was considered a respectable but mildly eccentric career choice.

He writes about technology, leadership and AI at Res·cog.
